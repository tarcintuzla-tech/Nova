# Code of Conduct
We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be kind, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and do not harass, discriminate or share others' private information.
Maintainers may remove contributions or block participants who violate these rules. Report problems to the maintainers via a private channel listed in the repository profile.
This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org); you may replace it with the full official version.
